Privacy policy

lifecal is a personal calendar tool used by its owner only.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, lifecal creates calendars in the owner's Google account and manages events in those calendars only (Google permission calendar.app.created). It has no access to other calendars, email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

What it stores

A sign-in token and a small sync record are kept on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server operated by lifecal — there is none.

Sharing

lifecal does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Data is used only to keep the owner's own calendar up to date. lifecal's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the calendars it created removes all of its data from Google.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/MysterioGhub/lifecal.
