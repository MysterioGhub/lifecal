lifecal

lifecal is a small personal tool that keeps its owner's own Google Calendar up to date. It fills three calendars that it creates itself — a daily routine, work duties and US market events — and keeps them in sync when the owner's schedule changes.

It is used by one person only, on their own computer, with their own Google account. It is not offered to other users and has no sign-up.

It can only access the calendars it created (Google permission calendar.app.created). It cannot see or change any other calendar or any other Google data.
